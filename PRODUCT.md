# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Parents and guardians who live near the school in Calabar and are choosing a school for a child between Creche and Primary age. Most visit on a phone. They want to know whether the school is safe, caring and disciplined, and how to get their child a place.

## Product Purpose
This is the public website for Pathfinder Elite International Schools, #9 Ediba Lane, off Ediba Road, Calabar Municipality, Cross River State. It introduces the school, its four sections and its services, shows the campus, and gets parents to make contact. A visit has succeeded when a parent calls or WhatsApps the Principal, or arranges to come and see the school.

## Positioning
- **Small and personal:** teachers know every child by name, and children stay at one school from Creche through Primary, keeping the same faces and routines as they move up.
- **Discipline and faith:** the school raises determined, disciplined and God-fearing learners of character, not only academic results.

## Operating Context
- Admissions happen by phone or WhatsApp with the Principal (+234 803 348 3918) and by visiting the campus behind the blue gates on Ediba Lane.
- Registration is ongoing into Creche, Reception, Nursery and Primary.
- Contact email: pathfinderinternational@gmail.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single static page: plain HTML and CSS, no build step (`index.html`, photos in `img/`, thumbnails in `img/thumb/`). Deployed on Vercel (https://pathfinder-elite-schools.vercel.app).
- Sections (classes): Creche, Reception, Nursery, Primary.
- Services: Jolly Phonics classes, dedicated staff, school bus services, after-school care, social clubs.
- Phone and WhatsApp are the real enquiry channel. The on-page enquiry form is not connected to any backend and is not planned to be. Decided 2026-11-21: it becomes a WhatsApp composer that opens a chat with the Principal, with the parent's details already typed into the message. It must never claim an enquiry was sent.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Pathfinder Elite International Schools. Short form: Pathfinder Elite.
- Motto: "Determination Personified".
- Vision: "To build a school that will stand the test of time by raising determined learners of character and excellence."
- Mission: "Raising determined, disciplined and God-fearing learners that will make a positive impact in our society."
- Logo: `img/logo.png`.
- The campus's blue gates are a real landmark parents use to find the school.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real campus photos in `img/`: gate and gate sign, street view, billboard, garden mural, playground, swings and see-saws, children at play.
- No testimonials, fees, staff profiles, exam results, accreditation details or enrolment numbers are on hand. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Every page leads to a call, a WhatsApp chat or a visit. These are the only real admissions channels.
2. Show the actual school (its gates, walls, playground and children) rather than generic education imagery.
3. Present care and discipline together. Parents should see a warm school that is also orderly and rooted in faith.
4. Keep it truthful and local. State facts the school can stand behind and make no claims beyond them.
5. Design for parents on phones first.
