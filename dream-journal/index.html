The school

Behind the blue gates, a small world of its own

Painted goldfish, a bee in glasses, and teachers who know every child by name.

Pathfinder Elite International Schools · #9 Ediba Lane, off Ediba Road

Walk past the three flags and the signboard and the walls turn sky blue and navy. A goldfish swims above the swings, a girl with a backpack runs across a green hill, and a bee in glasses waves from the gate. Inside, children in yellow check uniforms learn their sounds with Jolly Phonics, practise their numbers and queue for the swings.

Pathfinder Elite takes children from Creche through Primary. They move up through the same school with the same faces and routines, so no year starts among strangers.

“Raising determined, disciplined and God-fearing learners that will make a positive impact in our society.”
