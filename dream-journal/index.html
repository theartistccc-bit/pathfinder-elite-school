The school

Behind the blue gates, a small world of its own

Goldfish swim across the walls. A bee in glasses waves from the gate. And every teacher knows every child by name.

Pathfinder Elite takes children from Creche through Primary. They grow up in the same school with the same faces and routines, learning to read with Jolly Phonics, practising their numbers, and queueing happily for the swings.
