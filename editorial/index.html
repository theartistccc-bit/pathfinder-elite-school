The school

Behind the blue gates on Ediba Lane

A small school in Calabar that knows every child by name, and raises them on determination.

Pathfinder Elite International Schools · #9 Ediba Lane, off Ediba Road

Walk past the flags and the signboard and you find a world painted in sky blue and navy: a bee in glasses, a bell, a running girl with a backpack. Inside, children in yellow check uniforms learn their sounds with Jolly Phonics, practise their numbers and queue for the swings.

Pathfinder Elite takes children from Creche through Primary. They move up through the same building with the same faces and routines, so no year starts among strangers.

“Raising determined, disciplined and God-fearing learners that will make a positive impact in our society.”
